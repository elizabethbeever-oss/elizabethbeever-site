About Elizabeth

My professional background is in dentistry. I’m now developing my work at the intersection of healthtech, responsible AI, product development and digital clinical safety.

Through the BiteLabs UK Digital Health, AI & Innovation Fellowship and ongoing study in AI governance and clinical safety, I’m building a practical understanding of how healthcare technology is designed, evaluated and safely deployed.

I’m interested in the relationship between technology, ethics, safety and human needs, and in bringing curiosity, evidence and human judgement to the questions that arise when technology meets real healthcare settings.

Areas of interest

01

Responsible AI and evaluation

How do we know whether an AI tool is useful and safe in healthcare? I want to understand how evaluation, governance and accountability can work in everyday practice.

02

Healthcare product development and safety

How do real healthcare needs become well-designed products? This covers user needs, requirements, human factors and the clinical safety thinking that should shape a product from the start.

03

Human judgement, ethics and prevention

What might we overlook when technology meets human complexity? I’m drawn to where human judgement stays essential, who benefits from new tools, and how technology can support earlier, preventive care.
