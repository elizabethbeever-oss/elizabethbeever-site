Who is responsible

Elizabeth Beever is responsible for this website. For privacy enquiries or to exercise your rights, contact me through LinkedIn.

Optional analytics

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand readership and improve content. Google may process device and browser information, approximate location, page visits, referring pages, interaction events and cookie identifiers. Google receives your IP address when connecting to its service. Analytics is not required to use this website. Advertising personalisation is disabled in the site configuration. The lawful basis for optional analytics is consent under UK GDPR and PECR.

Google may process data outside the UK. See how Google uses information from sites using its services and its privacy policy for further information.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your analytics choice in local storage under site_analytics_consent_v1 for 180 days. This is necessary to remember your preference. If you accept, Google Analytics uses _ga and _ga_* cookies to distinguish visitors and maintain session information; the website configures these to expire after 180 days without automatic renewal. Google Analytics data retention is controlled separately in the analytics account.

Change your mind

Use Cookie settings in the footer on any page to reject analytics or change your preference. Withdrawal stops tracking on this site and removes accessible analytics cookies; it does not undo processing already carried out with consent.

Your rights
